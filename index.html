Entities are grouped only by the entity reference you assigned while tagging words — never by name-matching or guessing, and they can be story characters or general concepts alike. Discovered Classes are different: nobody assigns those — they emerge from how words actually behave (what relationships they take, what sits near them), and a word gets grouped the moment it has enough of that behavior, whether or not you ever gave it a Type. Relationship patterns are aggregated from every relationship you've taught, story and knowledge pooled together, and prediction on new sentences falls back through three tiers: exact word-pair, then free-text type-pair, then discovered-class-pair — which is how a totally novel word can still generalize, purely from behavior.

LM Studio is the teacher, not Pistol itself. MiniCPM5 2B Uncensored (or whatever model you select in LM Studio) analyzes each training sentence and returns structured linguistic labels. Pistol then feeds those labels through its existing sentence learner, rebuilds its grammar/sense/relationship indices, and stores the sentence as normal learned data. The teacher never writes Pistol's learned structures directly.
